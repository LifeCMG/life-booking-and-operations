Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

1. Purpose

Life Booking & Operations (“Life”) invests time in research, coordination, vendor communication, scheduling, and project preparation. This policy explains how cancellations, changes, deposits, completed work, and refunds are handled.

2. Before Work Begins

If Client cancels before Life begins substantive project work, Life will review the payment status and any administrative work already completed. Unless the project scope states otherwise, Life may refund unearned Life service fees, less any amount properly earned for agreed work already performed and any non-refundable charges authorized by Client.

3. After Work Begins

Once substantive work has begun, the service fee is earned as services are performed. If Client cancels, Life will determine the amount earned based on the work completed, time and project resources committed, deliverables provided, and the agreed scope. Any unearned portion of a prepaid Life service fee will be handled according to the written project terms and applicable law.

Life will not describe retained amounts as a punishment or penalty. Any amount retained is intended to reflect earned services, authorized costs, or contractual commitments.

4. Deposits and Payment Schedules

Life Full Service and custom projects may use deposits or milestone payments. The invoice and project scope will state whether a deposit is refundable, partially refundable, or earned upon specified work or milestones. No deposit will be treated as automatically non-refundable unless the applicable written terms clearly say so and such treatment is permitted by law.

5. Third-Party Costs

Hotels, transportation providers, venues, restaurants, ticketing companies, activity providers, and other vendors establish their own cancellation and refund policies. Life cannot promise a refund from a third party. Client is responsible for third-party cancellation fees, non-refundable purchases, deposits, or other vendor charges that Client approved.

6. Changes Instead of Cancellation

Life may be able to modify a project instead of canceling it. Included changes depend on the selected package. Material changes - such as new dates, destinations, vendors, attendee counts, or project objectives - may require additional fees or a revised scope. Life will disclose any additional Life fee before performing chargeable additional work.

7. Cancellation by Life

Life may suspend or cancel services for nonpayment, lack of required Client cooperation, unlawful or unsafe requests, abusive conduct, material misrepresentation, or another material breach. Any refund will be determined based on earned services, authorized costs, the project terms, and applicable law.

If Life cancels for reasons not caused by Client breach, Life will review any prepaid service fee and refund any unearned portion as appropriate. Third-party refunds remain subject to vendor rules.

8. How to Cancel

Cancellation requests should be sent in writing to lifebookingoperations@gmail.com. The request should include the Client name, project name, and requested cancellation date. The effective cancellation date is the date Life receives the written request, unless the parties agree otherwise.

9. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will ordinarily be returned through the original payment method when practical. Payment processors and banks may require additional processing time. Life does not control the time a bank or payment processor takes to post a refund.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to lifebookingoperations@gmail.com.
