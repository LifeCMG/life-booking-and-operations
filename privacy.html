Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Life Booking & Operations (“Life,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of visitors, prospective clients, and clients. This Privacy Policy explains the information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Information you submit through our consultation or contact forms, such as your name, email address, requested service, project or event date, and project details.

Information you provide during consultations or active projects, including preferences, schedules, participant or group details, budgets, approvals, and vendor-related information.

Transaction and invoice information associated with payments. Payment-card and bank credentials are processed by our payment processor rather than collected through our ordinary website consultation form.

Communications you send to us by email or other approved channels.

Technical information that may be generated by our hosting, form, security, or payment providers, such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, and security logs.

3. How We Use Information

Respond to inquiries and consultation requests.

Evaluate, quote, plan, coordinate, and deliver requested services.

Communicate about reservations, schedules, vendors, invoices, approvals, changes, and project status.

Maintain business, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution records.

Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, or unauthorized activity.

Comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Improve our services and customer experience.

4. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate portions of our business. Our current workflow may include Formspree for website form submissions, Square for invoices and payment processing, Google Workspace for business communications, Vercel or another hosting provider for the website, and vendors selected for a Client project. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Form submissions may be stored within the Formspree account and may also be delivered to our designated business email. Payment information is handled through Square’s payment environment rather than through the ordinary consultation form.

5. When We Share Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, including with hotels, restaurants, venues, transportation providers, activity providers, event vendors, or other service providers. We seek to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant coordination.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, to investigate fraud or misuse, or in connection with a business reorganization or transfer where legally permitted.

6. Information About Other People

If you provide information about another traveler, guest, employee, participant, or other person, you represent that you are authorized to provide the information for the relevant project. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not necessary for us to perform the requested service.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for inquiries, active projects, business records, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security, and legitimate operational needs. Retention periods may vary by type of information and service provider.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to our size and operations, and we rely on reputable service providers for hosting, forms, communications, and payments. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not submit Social Security numbers, passwords, full payment-card details, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through the ordinary consultation form.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website or its service providers may use essential technical storage, cookies, logs, or similar technologies necessary for hosting, security, form functionality, or payment functionality. If we later add non-essential analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies, we will update this policy and implement any notices or choices required by applicable law.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted directly to Life, subject to legal, accounting, contractual, security, and record-retention requirements. Requests should be sent to lifebookingoperations@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

11. Children

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children under 13 through our consultation form. A parent, guardian, organization, or authorized adult should handle any project involving minors.

12. External Services and Links

Our website or communications may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are controlled by them, not by Life. Review their policies before providing information or making a purchase.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, website, vendors, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted with a revised effective date.
