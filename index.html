Scope & Service Agreement

Know exactly what you are purchasing before work begins.

The agreement is the written project plan between the client and Life Booking & Operations. It protects both sides by defining what is included before time or money is committed.

Project scope

Client name, project dates, group size, requested services, specific bookings or logistics Life will coordinate, deliverables and expected timeline.

Money & approvals

Life service fee, required deposit or full payment, third-party costs, who pays each vendor, spending approvals and when additional work requires a new quote.

Changes & cancellation

Included revision rounds, what counts as out-of-scope work, cancellation timing, refunds, vendor cancellation rules and what happens to work already completed.

Important: A consultation request or verbal “go ahead” is not authorization for Life to begin paid work. The project becomes active only after the written scope is accepted and the required payment has been received.
How Payment Works

Your Life service fee and your booking costs are separate.

This separation keeps pricing clear: you pay Life for coordination, while hotels, transportation companies, venues, restaurants, ticket providers and other vendors charge for their own products and services.

1. Pay Life for the service

After the scope is accepted, Life sends an invoice for the required service fee or deposit. Basic and Plus projects may require the full service fee before work begins. Larger Full Service or custom projects may use a deposit and scheduled balance payments as stated in the agreement.

Scope accepted → required payment received → project becomes active.

2. Pay third parties for bookings

Whenever practical, clients pay hotels, transportation providers, venues, ticket sellers and other vendors directly through the vendor's secure checkout or authorized payment process. Life coordinates the details without treating those third-party costs as Life revenue.

Life should never ask a client to email or place full card numbers in the consultation form. Payment details should be handled through secure payment providers.

Changes, Cancellation & Refunds

A fair policy should cover work performed—not punish the client.

Instead of an arbitrary cancellation penalty, the agreement should clearly state when a project can be cancelled, what portion of the service fee is refundable and how completed work is treated.

Before work begins

If the client cancels before Life begins work, the agreement can specify whether the service payment is refundable, less any clearly disclosed non-refundable costs already incurred.

After work begins

Once research, coordination or other agreed work has started, the agreement can provide that fees attributable to work already performed are earned and not refundable, with any remaining refund determined by the written policy.

Third-party cancellations

Hotels, tickets, transportation and other vendor purchases remain subject to each vendor's own cancellation and refund terms. Life cannot guarantee a third party will issue a refund.

Extra work

Requests beyond the approved scope should be quoted and approved before additional work begins rather than silently added to the original package.

This website summarizes the intended business process; the final customer agreement and refund/cancellation language should be reviewed for your actual business structure and applicable law before launch.

What Life Is

Your coordination partner when a plan has too many moving pieces.

Life Booking & Operations is a booking, logistics and coordination service for individuals, groups, events and organizations. You tell us what needs to happen; we help research, organize and coordinate the agreed details through one clear point of contact.

What we actually do

We can help coordinate accommodations, restaurant and activity reservations, transportation, schedules, vendor research, confirmations, group logistics and master itineraries. The exact scope depends on the package or custom project you choose.

“You have the plan. Life helps organize the details behind it.”

What Life is not

Life does not own the hotels, restaurants, airlines, transportation providers or venues used in a project. Third-party charges are normally paid separately from the Life service fee. Complex projects, large groups and unusually high-volume requests receive a custom scope and quote.

Our value is the time, organization, research, communication and coordination required to bring multiple services together.
